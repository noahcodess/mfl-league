# Player Development

Noah's rules for how players grow, peak, decline and retire (Nov 9). Where these differ from the old rulebook, these win.

## Potential and scouting

- Every player has a hidden **potential** (max star rating he can reach).
- You can always see your own players' potential. Other teams' players and draft prospects: only by **scouting**.
- Scouts: 5 for free agents; for the draft, 2 per 1st-round pick and 1 per 2nd- or 3rd-round pick you own. Scouting never notifies the commissioner. (Replaces the old "5 scouts before the draft and midseason" rule.)
- Draft classes: about 4 five-star-potential prospects at most per draft. The league is top-heavy right now and will thin out as current stars retire.

## Growth

- Rookies enter at **age 21**, below their potential (see open question 1).
- **Offseason boost:** +1 star every offseason until potential is reached.
- **In-season development:** about +0.5 star on average, up to +1 for a great rookie season.
- So a rookie can gain about 1.5 stars in his first season plus offseason. Example: 5-star potential enters at 2.5, reaches 4.0 after one offseason, and hits full potential around age 23 to 24.
- Ratings never go above potential.

## Decline

- **Age 25:** only underperformers decline (weak stats, poor play). Good players hold.
- **Age 26 and up:** everyone declines. A good player typically loses 0.5 to 1 star a year.
- Max decline: 2 stars (see open question 2).
- Declining players show a **red star / red half star** on the site.
- Different ratings fall at different speeds: speed, catching and awareness drop more than others.

## Retirement

- Players retire between **26 and 28** (the old rulebook said 26 to 27).
- Retired players can have their jersey retired or go into the team hall of fame.

## Open questions

1. Rookies enter "2 stars below potential", but the example has a 5-star-potential rookie entering at 2.5 (2.5 below). Which is it, or is it a range?
2. Is the 2-star max decline per offseason or over a whole career?
3. What counts as "not doing well" at 25: stats vs position average, team record, or a commissioner call?
