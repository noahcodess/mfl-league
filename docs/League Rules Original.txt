8 ~ Activity - NISH RULE

HC's in both the MFL & MBA must be active in either one or both leagues, active meaning joining game events, chatting with others in chats, making moves and trades, and asking any questions about your team and the league. Failure to do any of the following will result in a downgrade from Head Coach to Fan and the HC position for that team will be open for others to claim.

9 ~ Head Coaching for Both Leagues

HC's from now on must head coach both their biomes teams in both MFL & MBA. Meaning if you own a team in the MFL, you must also own the team in the same biome in the MBA. There can no longer be dual enrollment coaches for one biome.

10 ~ Signing & Trading Back-to-Back

Teams can no longer sign players off free agency and trade them immediately after for picks and other necessities for their team, if made. There will be a punishment for that team.

11 ~ Scout Limit

Each team will have 5 scouts in both before the draft and in the middle of the season. If any team goes over the limit at any point and uses it to their advantage, they will be penalized.

12 ~ Trade Accounts

As of April 2nd, trades will officially be approved by both teams as well as now officially the league office to make sure all trades are fair and not let unbelievable unfair trades occur, trades won't be entirely fair but it disables insanely unfair trades and we'll let both head coaches know if the trade has been approved by the league office or not.

13 ~ Coach Weekly Check-In's
